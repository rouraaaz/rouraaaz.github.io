Beyond the CV: who I am and what drives me.

I consider myself a curious, active person with solid values. I enjoy traveling, meeting people and discovering new cultures, especially understanding how other people approach situations and problems from perspectives different from ours. I also like staying active and playing sports, especially skiing and padel.

I've always been curious to learn, explore new ideas and put them into practice. That attitude has led me, during my internships, to get involved in small projects that weren't originally part of my responsibilities. I had the chance to collaborate directly with the head of the office on different initiatives, which let me take on new challenges, broaden my knowledge and grow both professionally and personally.

I'm motivated by challenges and environments where I can keep learning, step out of my comfort zone and surround myself with people I can learn from.
