Sneaker Care Co.: Student Company CFO

Ran the finance function of a registered 7-person student company.

Context

Students at HZ set up and run a real, registered company. I was the CFO of Sneaker Care Co. in Vlissingen, Jan–Jul 2024.

Problem

With €350 of starting capital, the team needed to know at all times what it could afford.

What I did

  • Built a linked balance sheet, P&L and cash-flow model in Excel.
  • Managed the budget and tracked actuals against plan.
  • Set up the bank-to-ledger reconciliation and prepared the tax documentation.

Result

  • ~€1,500 revenue in 3 months.
  • Books reconciled and tax documentation prepared.
